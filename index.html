    Company shall not be liable to provide the replacement for an absorbed candidate in the following cases:
  • o

    If the client does not ask for the replacement of the same profile within 08 Days from the date of the absorbed candidate’s resignation or the absorbed candidate’s last working date in the case of the absconded candidate.
